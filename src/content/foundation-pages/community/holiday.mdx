## **Celebrate the Season with Interledger! Participate in Our Holiday Card and Sticker Contest.**

<br />
As we wrap up another year of growing open financial systems, we want to
celebrate the creativity of our global community. We are calling on all artists,
builders, and community members to design our official 2026 End-of-Year Holiday
Card and Sticker set. Winning designs will be printed and mailed directly to
Interledger members across the globe in December!

<br />

</Paragraph>

<Paragraph>

### **Eligibility**

The contest is open to the entire Interledger community. Submitting a design serves as an official qualifying activity toward Interledger Foundation Membership. While anyone can participate and submit designs, only registered members are eligible to receive the physical badge and end-of-year holiday package.

</Paragraph>

<Paragraph>

### **Categories & Design Specs**

Entries are accepted across two main categories: **Holiday Sticker** and **Holiday Card**. To ensure inclusive holiday messaging across global cultures, all submissions must align with an inclusive "end of year" theme without Christmas-specific imagery or text. Design files should be uploaded in .PDF, .AI, or .PNG format at 300 DPI.

We have pre-sized templates here: [Design Kit](https://drive.google.com/drive/u/2/folders/16dp5TFcuosKAA3gZsaIO34AON1m44TsT)

- **Holiday Card:**

  - Dimensions: 4" x 6" (or 6" x 4").
  - Format: Double-sided layout.
  - Bleed & Margins: 3mm bleed on all sides, CMYK color mode, minimum 300 DPI.

- **Holiday Sticker:**

  - Format: Vector path die-cut or standard geometric shape (e.g., 2" x 2" or max 3.21" x 2").
  - Finish Compatibility: High-resolution CMYK, compatible with matte vinyl printing.

### **Designer Inspiration & Baseline Reference**

- Need inspiration? Check out baseline design kits created by our team, featuring sample compositions and color inspiration: [Design Kit](https://drive.google.com/drive/u/2/folders/16dp5TFcuosKAA3gZsaIO34AON1m44TsT).
- Baseline templates and layout guides can be downloaded directly from the design kit repository: [Design Kit](https://drive.google.com/drive/u/2/folders/16dp5TFcuosKAA3gZsaIO34AON1m44TsT).

</Paragraph>

<Paragraph>

### **Timeline**

A judging panel from our team will select the top 5 finalists, followed by a public community vote to choose the final winning designs. Key campaign milestones include:

- **Oct 26:** Submissions open
- **Nov 11:** Community Call showcase and final submission push
- **Nov 13:** Submissions close
- **Nov 16–17:** Internal judging panel selects 5 finalists
- **Nov 17–23:** Community voting window
- **Dec 1:** Winners announced
- **Dec 5:** Print order sent

</Paragraph>

<Paragraph>

### **Judging Criteria & Selection Process**

Submissions will be evaluated through a two-stage selection process.

#### **Stage 1: Internal Panel Screening**

We will review all eligible entries and select 5 finalists per category based on the following rubric:<br /> 

| **Criterion**                | **Weight** | **Evaluation Focus**                                                                              |
| ---------------------------- | ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Theme Alignment**          | 30%        | Captures end-of-year spirit, inclusivity, and global community connection without religious bias. |
| **Creativity & Originality** | 30%        | Unique visual interpretation, compelling composition, and strong personal artistic expression.    |
| **Print Feasibility**        | 20%        | Meets technical specifications (300 DPI, bleed lines, clarity when scaled).                       |
| **Versatility & Appeal**     | 20%        | Translates effectively across physical print formats and digital showcase spaces.                 |

#### **Stage 2: Community Voting**

- The top 5 finalist designs in each category will be published for public community voting.
- Voting will open on November 17 and close on November 23.
- Community members will vote via [Slack](https://join.slack.com/t/interledger/shared_invite/zt-44g089zrn-XdSIiHF~cs8Oo_MBmSfECA), [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/interledger-foundation) and [X](https://x.com/Interledger).<br />

</Paragraph>

<Paragraph>

### **Official Competition Rules & Policies**

#### **Theme & Cultural Inclusivity**

- **Theme:** "End of Year Celebration".
- Designs must focus on global reflection, gratitude, community, and looking forward to the new year.
- **Exclusions:** Avoid specific religious-only imagery to ensure the designs are inclusive across cultures, holidays, and secular celebrations worldwide.

#### **AI Usage Policy**

In alignment with the Interledger Foundation Programmatic AI Policies:

- **Permitted Support:** AI tools (e.g., Midjourney, DALL-E, Adobe Firefly, ChatGPT) are permitted as support tools for brainstorming, concept drafting, design creation, or refining ideas.
- **Value on Originality:** We value creative thinking and original artistic vision. Submissions relying primarily or entirely on unedited generative AI outputs will be accepted, but evaluated negatively against original human artwork.
- **Mandatory Disclosure:** Every submission must include a brief disclosure stating whether AI tools were used, which tools were employed, and how they contributed to the final piece (e.g., initial ideation vs. final asset generation).

### **Intellectual Property & Printing Rights**

By submitting a design, participants grant the Interledger Foundation full permission to print, reproduce, and distribute the submitted artwork for the holiday campaign mailers and promotional materials.

- **Ownership:** Submitting artists retain full ownership and copyright of their original creative assets.
- **Licensing:** By entering, participants grant the Interledger Foundation a perpetual, non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license to reproduce, print, distribute, and display the submitted designs in physical mailings, digital assets, and promotional media.
- **Open Distribution:** Final selected designs will be shared under a Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY 4.0) license with full artist attribution.
